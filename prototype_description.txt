## 1. Upstream PM alert

Use something like this:

* **Page title:** `Potential downstream dependency`

* **Subtext:** `We found a likely impact from your Checkout Status Redesign on Revenue Analytics.`

* **Dependency visual:**
  `Checkout Platform  →  Revenue Analytics`
  Small arrow label: `payment status data`

* **Severity indicator:** `High impact`

* **Confidence indicator:** `87% confidence`

* **Feedback control beside confidence:** `Accurate` / `Not accurate`

* **Proposed change**
  `Checkout is replacing payment_pending and payment_failed with a consolidated payment status model.`
  `Target launch: September 21`

* **Why Revenue Analytics may be affected**
  `Revenue Analytics currently uses these payment status values to calculate checkout conversion and payment-failure metrics.`

* **Likely downstream work**
  `• Update event and status mappings`
  `• Update dashboard calculation logic`
  `• Validate historical and new reporting remain comparable`
  `Estimated effort: 3–5 engineering days`

* **Evidence**
  `Checkout Redesign PRD · “Replace legacy payment states…”`
  `Revenue Metrics Spec · References payment_pending and payment_failed`
  `Checkout Eng Sync · “Targeting September 21 rollout”`
  Each is a clickable source chip/link.

* **Outreach preview**
  `Revenue Analytics should be made aware before the payment model is finalized.`
  `They’ll receive a brief explaining the proposed change, likely impact, evidence, and estimated work.`

* **Primary button:** `Approve outreach`

* **Secondary actions:** `Edit impact` · `Already coordinated` · `Dismiss`

After **Approve outreach**, show a lightweight confirmation: `Revenue Analytics has been invited to review this dependency.`

## 2. Upstream dependency review

* **Detected downstream dependency:** impacted team/product, why it may be affected, and confidence level.
* **Impact details:** likely downstream changes required, estimated effort, and key risks if coordination happens late.
* **Supporting evidence:** snippets/links from PRDs, meetings, code, or analytics definitions that support the inference.
* **Actions:** **Approve outreach** as primary CTA, plus edit impact summary, dismiss dependency, or mark as already coordinated.

## 3. Downstream impact brief

Use this:

* **Page title:** `Review potential impact`

* **Subtext:** `Checkout Platform is considering a change that may affect Revenue Analytics.`

* **Dependency visual:**
  `Checkout Platform  →  Revenue Analytics`
  Arrow label: `payment status data`

* **Status badge:** `Proposed change`

* **Target timing:** `Target launch: September 21`

* **What’s changing**
  `Checkout plans to replace payment_pending and payment_failed with a consolidated payment status model.`
  `The design is still being finalized.`

* **Why this may affect you**
  `Your reporting pipeline currently uses these payment status values to calculate checkout conversion and payment-failure metrics.`

* **Potential impact**
  `If the change ships as proposed, Revenue Analytics may need to:`
  `• Update event and status mappings`
  `• Update dashboard calculation logic`
  `• Validate that historical and new reporting remain comparable`

* **Impact summary card:**
  `Estimated effort: 3–5 engineering days`
  `Severity: High`
  `Confidence: 87%`

* **Feedback control beside estimate:**
  `Estimate looks right` / `Needs adjustment`

* **Evidence**
  Clickable source chips:

  * `Checkout Redesign PRD`
  * `Revenue Metrics Spec`
  * `Checkout Eng Sync`

  Clicking a source expands the relevant excerpt rather than opening an entirely new workflow.

* **What we need from you**
  `Please confirm whether this impact looks accurate and flag anything the Checkout team should account for before the design is finalized.`

* **Primary button:** `Confirm impact`

* **Secondary buttons:** `Flag additional risk` · `Request discussion` · `Not impacted`

* **After “Confirm impact”:**
  `Impact confirmed. Checkout Platform will see your response before finalizing the change.`

## Persistent top bar and navigation

Use a persistent **top bar with a persona/view switcher**: `Upstream PM: Maya` | `Downstream PM: Leo`, plus a simple three-step progress rail: `1. Email alert → 2. Dependency review → 3. Downstream impact brief`. When the user clicks **Review dependency** in the email, automatically move to step 2; when they click **Approve outreach**, automatically switch to the downstream PM view and open step 3, with a small banner saying `Now viewing Leo’s experience`. Also let panelists click any step or persona at any time, so they can freely inspect the whole flow without getting lost.
